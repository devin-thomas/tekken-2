Tekken 2 Recompiled changelog
=============================

0.1.0 (first release)
---------------------

The first release of Tekken 2 Recompiled, a native Mac and Windows
version of Namco's Tekken 2 for the PlayStation.

- Plays Tekken 2 (USA) (Rev 1) from your own BIN/CUE disc image, started
  with the included OpenBIOS. The first start asks for the disc and
  remembers it.
- Mac: an app for Apple silicon Macs, macOS 11 or later.
- Windows: a 64-bit app for Windows 10 and 11 that keeps its settings and
  memory cards in your user folder, not in the install folder.
- Every connected controller and the keyboard work at once, with hot-plug.
  PlayStation controllers map one to one; other controllers map by button
  position.
- A settings panel (Select held for a second, F1 or Esc): each player's
  controls, swapping players, internal resolution, fullscreen, audio
  latency, an FPS display, and the mode.
- Two modes: Enhanced, the default, loads faster and never drops frames;
  Original keeps the PlayStation's loading times and slowdowns.
- Fullscreen with F11, Alt+Enter or a double-click on the picture.
- The 3D fighters draw correctly: a fix in the recompiler for a
  coprocessor load inside a branch delay slot, which had stretched the
  character models into spikes.
